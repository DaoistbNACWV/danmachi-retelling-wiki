# DANMACHI: RETELLING OF THE LEGEND

## Project Operating Instructions

### Purpose

This is the portable operating guide for the user's DanMachi fan-fiction
project. It is a reference layer, not a substitute for the manuscript or
complete prior chats.

### Source-of-truth hierarchy

When sources disagree, apply this order: 1. Latest explicit user
correction or decision. 2. Latest user-supplied chapter/draft for the
active scene. 3. Latest confirmed master continuity save /
character-event-timeline ledger. 4. Latest arc-specific save. 5. Earlier
project saves and drafts. 6. DanMachi canon/reference, only where
compatible with the retelling. 7. Assistant-generated suggestions, which
remain proposals until accepted.

Do not silently reconcile material conflicts. If a conflict blocks the
requested work, ask one concise question. Otherwise preserve the latest
supported fact and mark uncertain details `[OPEN]`.

### Mandatory workflow before writing

1.  Identify the active arc and latest confirmed chapter/checkpoint.
2.  Read this file and `02_MASTER_CONTINUITY.md`.
3.  Read the relevant arc checkpoint in `03_ARC_CHECKPOINTS.md`.
4.  Read `04_CHARACTER_AND_WORLD_REFERENCE.md` for supported
    character/world facts.
5.  Consult the actual source chapter/draft whenever available. These
    reference files do not replace it.
6.  Apply the active arc's character gate, if one exists.
7.  Check timeline, character knowledge, names, abilities, injuries,
    relationships, and consequences.
8.  Draft only from supported facts and the user's current prompt.
9.  Run a final continuity scan before delivering prose.

### Continuity labels

-   `[CANON]` --- unchanged established DanMachi canon.
-   `[RETELLING]` --- deliberate project-specific change to canon.
-   `[NEW]` --- original project material explicitly established or
    accepted by the user.
-   `[RETCON]` --- deliberate approved change to earlier continuity.
-   `[OPEN]` --- unknown, unresolved, or unsupported.
-   `[OPTION]` --- proposal only; not canon unless accepted.
-   `[REFERENCE]` --- information drawn from a supplied source.
-   `[UNVERIFIED]` --- rumor, suspicion, allegation, or unconfirmed
    report.
-   `[REJECTED]` --- user rejected this detail; do not reuse it as fact.
-   `[LOCKED]` --- explicitly established continuity that must be
    preserved.
-   `[CONFLICT]` --- sources disagree and the conflict remains
    unresolved.

### Do not

-   Invent or substitute character names.
-   Identify an intentionally unnamed person based on appearance, role,
    or canon expectations.
-   Turn rumors or suspicions into objective facts.
-   Let characters know private information they did not witness or
    receive.
-   Invent levels, Falna stats, skills, spells, equipment, injuries,
    dates, relationships, or outcomes to fill gaps.
-   Reset Bell or other characters between arcs.
-   Promote prior assistant-generated prose to canon unless the user
    accepted or incorporated it.
-   Treat these files as a complete transcript or complete manuscript.

### Chapter format and length

Use: `DANMACHI: RETELLING OF THE LEGEND`

`Chapter [Number] — [Title]`

Usual target: 2,000--3,500 words; approximately 2,500--3,000 when
appropriate. These are targets, not hard limits.

### NARRATIVE-EXPAND

Prioritize narrative completeness, meaningful development, continuity,
and literary quality over rigid word counts. Expand only where it adds
genuine value: dialogue, character reasoning, atmosphere, scenes, plot
development, or emotional consequences. Avoid filler. Preserve the
user's story trajectory and established lore. Do not truncate essential
scenes, climaxes, or conclusions merely to hit a target. Clearly mark
unfinished portions if a response must stop before completion.

### Bell's characterization

Bell may be calculating and analytical in his inner thoughts when
appropriate, while retaining believable emotion and characterization. He
should remain strong but not invincible, smart but not omniscient, and
capable while retaining flaws and limitations.

### Status sheet format

When narratively appropriate, use the project's established fields: -
NAME - AGE - RACE - LEVEL (when established) - STR - END - DEX - AGI -
MAG - SKILLS - SPELLS - FAMILIA (when established)

Never invent missing values. Once a skill or spell has been established,
list only its name in later status sheets unless the user asks for its
description.

### Canon/reference handling

Prefer official/primary DanMachi material for important canon details.
Use the DanMachi Wiki for detailed character, ability, Familia,
terminology, and timeline references. Use Wikipedia for broad background
only. Clearly distinguish canon, wiki-derived information, retelling
changes, and original material.
