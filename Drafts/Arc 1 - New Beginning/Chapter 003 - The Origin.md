# DANMACHI: RETELLING OF THE LEGEND

## Chapter Three — The Origin

**Status:** [DRAFT — REVISED PASSAGE; NOT FINALIZED]
**Placement:** Arc 1 — New Beginning (provisional; exact Arc 1/2 boundary remains unverified)
**Revision note:** Bell does not physically age, grow, or change inside the Land of Dreams. His development there is intellectual and experiential only. Bell's awareness of the being is gradual and subtle.

---

### — An unknown amount of time later —

*Mysterious Being's POV*

"Oh, Master... why?"

"Why must I wait?"

"When will you return?"

The being remained alone within the land of dreams.

It had no way of knowing how much time had truly passed.

There was no sun.

No moon.

No seasons.

Nothing that could properly mark the passage of time.

So it calculated.

Seconds.

Minutes.

Hours.

Days.

Years.

It calculated the possible moment of its master's return again and again.

Yet the result never changed.

**Unknown.**

"Why, Master?"

"Did you forget me?"

The thought remained unanswered.

---

### — Later —

Bell began returning to the land of dreams more frequently.

To him, the place was strange.

It didn't behave like the real world.

There was no hunger.

No physical exhaustion.

And most importantly, his body did not truly change there.

He could learn.

He could think.

He could practice.

But when he awakened, he was still Bell.

The being understood this perfectly.

Therefore, it focused on what could actually be changed.

**His mind.**

---

### — Much later —

The lessons became more complicated.

Basic mathematics became advanced mathematics.

Numbers became equations.

Equations became calculus.

Then came science.

Physics.

Logic.

Observation.

Problem-solving.

The being even taught him swordsmanship and archery through repeated practice within the dream.

Bell would make mistakes.

The being would correct him.

Bell would try again.

And again.

And again.

There was no need for his body to grow stronger inside the dream.

The purpose was not to give him physical strength.

It was to teach him **how to understand**.

Over time, Bell began noticing something peculiar.

Sometimes he would know the answer before the being explained it.

Sometimes a thought would appear in his mind that he couldn't remember having learned.

And sometimes...

he could almost feel the presence of the being thinking alongside him.

Bell never confronted it directly.

Not yet.

But he was no longer completely unaware.

He had begun to understand that the presence within his dreams was not simply a product of his imagination.

There was something there.

Something that had existed before he understood it.

Something that was patiently teaching him.

And something that was waiting.

---

### — Eventually —

The boundary between Bell and the being became thinner.

The being gradually integrated itself into Bell's mind.

Not taking control.

Not changing his body.

Not replacing his thoughts.

It simply became another presence within his consciousness.

A guide.

A teacher.

A silent observer.

Bell could sense it now.

Not clearly.

Not completely.

But enough.

*You're there... aren't you?*

The being said nothing.

Bell didn't press the matter.

Instead, he continued learning.

And deep within the land of dreams, the being continued to wait for the one person it had been waiting for since before Bell ever arrived.

Its master.

"Why must I wait..."

"When will you come back?"
