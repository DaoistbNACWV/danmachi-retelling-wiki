# DANMACHI: RETELLING OF THE LEGEND

## Chapter Eighty-Three — Night Life

Night had settled over Melen.

The city gradually became quieter as the last traces of daylight disappeared beyond the horizon.

Along the coastline, the sea reflected the pale light of the moon.

The campsite was illuminated by a small magical lantern.

Bell sat beside the table, examining a few notes.

Ein was nearby.

She had spent most of the evening organizing their supplies, although her attention had gradually shifted toward something considerably more interesting.

Him.

"Bell."

"Hm?"

"You're still working?"

He glanced at her.

"Just checking a few things."

"You've been checking those things for nearly an hour."

"Really?"

"Yes."

He looked at the notes.

Then at the clock.

"...Oh."

Ein crossed her arms.

"You're doing it again."

"Doing what?"

"Getting distracted."

Bell smiled.

"That's a rather unfortunate accusation."

"Is it inaccurate?"

He considered the question.

Then closed the notebook.

"No."

Ein laughed.

"At least you're honest."

Bell stood and approached her.

For a moment, they simply looked at each other.

Their relationship had changed considerably since the purification.

Not because their feelings had suddenly appeared.

They had already existed.

Rather, something that had once stood between them was finally gone.

Ein was free.

She could make her own decisions without wondering whether an invisible influence was interfering with her thoughts.

She could laugh without fear.

She could be close to him without questioning whether her feelings were truly her own.

And Bell, who had spent so much time trying to understand the nature of her condition, was gradually learning something rather different.

He didn't always need to understand everything.

Sometimes, he simply needed to be there.

Ein reached for his hand.

"You're thinking again."

"Am I?"

"Yes."

"That's unfortunate."

She smiled.

"Very."

He gently pulled her closer.

Their foreheads touched.

For a moment, neither spoke.

Then Ein wrapped her arms around him.

Bell returned the embrace.

It was a quiet expression of affection, but neither was in a hurry to let go.

When Ein finally looked up, her cheeks were slightly warm.

"You're smiling."

"Am I not allowed to?"

She narrowed her eyes.

"You're using my words again."

"Perhaps."

Ein laughed and rested her head against his chest.

The conversation gradually became unnecessary.

There were no experiments waiting for them.

No instruments requiring attention.

No urgent discoveries demanding another sleepless night.

Only the two of them.

Bell gently brushed a strand of hair away from her face.

"You're happy?"

Ein looked at him.

Then smiled.

"Very."

He returned the smile.

"Good."

She leaned against him again.

For a while, they remained that way, listening to the distant waves.

Eventually, Ein looked up.

"Bell?"

"Yes?"

"Can we forget about the investigation tonight?"

He glanced toward the table.

Then toward the sea.

Finally, he looked at her.

"...I suppose we can."

"You're hesitating."

"I'm considering the consequences."

She gave him an unimpressed look.

"Bell."

He laughed.

"All right. No investigations."

Ein seemed satisfied.

She took his hand and led him toward the sleeping arrangements.

The magical barrier surrounding the campsite was already active. It provided privacy and protection against disturbances from outside.

Bell glanced at it.

"Convenient."

Ein looked over her shoulder.

"Did you prepare that specifically?"

"Partially."

"Partially?"

"Security is important."

She stared at him.

Then burst into laughter.

"You're impossible."

"That's what I've been told."

The lantern eventually dimmed.

Outside, the waves continued their gentle rhythm.

Inside, the two settled down together, sharing the quiet comfort of a night without experiments, interruptions, or obligations.

Bell lay awake for a little while, listening to Ein's breathing.

She was here.

Safe.

And free to choose the life she wanted.

He glanced at her.

Ein had already closed her eyes, though the faint smile on her face suggested she wasn't quite asleep.

"Good night, Ein."

"Good night, Bell."

Tomorrow could wait.

For once, Bell didn't mind.

**End of Chapter Eighty-Three.**
