# DANMACHI: RETELLING OF THE LEGEND

## Chapter Eighty-One — The Girls

There were many ways to travel.

Walking was economical.

Riding a carriage was comfortable.

And traveling with an unnecessarily large collection of luggage was apparently a lifestyle.

The Loki Familia expedition had chosen the third option.

"Careful with that!"

"Don't put it there!"

"That's my bag!"

"Then perhaps you should have packed less!"

The carriage nearly came to a halt.

Riveria closed her eyes.

She was beginning to regret agreeing to supervise this expedition.

The luggage had become a logistical problem.

Clothing occupied a considerable portion of the available space. There were additional accessories, personal necessities, spare equipment, and several items that Riveria couldn't immediately identify.

She looked toward the nearest pile.

"Why do you need three different dresses?"

One of the girls smiled.

"Different occasions, Riveria-sama."

"We're going to the beach."

"Exactly!"

Riveria stared.

She decided not to pursue the matter.

There were more important things to worry about.

Or at least, she hoped there were.

The expedition was supposed to be relatively straightforward. Investigate the surrounding coastline, inspect the Leviathan seal, and determine whether there were any suspicious activities.

It was a reasonable assignment.

Unfortunately, the girls seemed to have interpreted it as an opportunity for a vacation.

Their excitement was practically contagious.

Conversations about swimming, sunbathing, food, and shopping continued throughout the journey.

Some were already discussing which beach they wanted to visit.

Others were comparing their swimsuits.

Riveria watched the entire spectacle with an expression that suggested she was reconsidering her life choices.

Then there were three exceptions.

Ais.

Alicia.

And Lefiya.

Unlike the others, they weren't particularly enthusiastic.

Ais sat quietly beside the carriage window.

Her golden eyes followed the passing scenery.

Lefiya occasionally glanced outside, though her attention was clearly elsewhere.

Alicia maintained a pleasant expression, but even she seemed somewhat distracted.

It wasn't difficult to understand why.

There was someone missing.

Someone they would have preferred to accompany them.

"Something bothering you three?"

Riveria's question drew their attention.

Ais shook her head.

"No."

Lefiya immediately nodded.

"Nothing at all, Riveria-sama!"

Alicia smiled.

"Everything is perfectly fine."

Riveria looked at them.

Then at the other girls.

Then back at them.

"...I see."

She didn't believe a single word.

Ais returned her attention to the window.

She remembered Bell.

His peculiar way of thinking.

His habit of making observations that other people overlooked.

And the occasional smile he gave her whenever she said something unexpected.

She wondered what he was doing.

Perhaps he was investigating something.

Perhaps he was conducting another experiment.

Or perhaps he was simply enjoying himself somewhere.

That last possibility made her slightly unhappy.

Not because she didn't want him to enjoy himself.

Rather, she wished she could be there.

Beside him.

Lefiya was having similar thoughts.

Although hers were considerably less peaceful.

Why wasn't he coming?

Was he busy?

Did he have other responsibilities?

Had he forgotten about them?

No.

That was unlikely.

Probably.

She shook her head.

Alicia noticed.

"Something on your mind, Lefiya?"

"N-No!"

Alicia smiled knowingly.

"Bell, perhaps?"

Lefiya immediately turned red.

"W-Why would you bring him up?!"

"Because you've been thinking about him for the past twenty minutes."

"I HAVE NOT!"

Several heads turned toward them.

Riveria sighed.

Ais blinked.

Alicia simply laughed.

Lefiya covered her face.

This was becoming unbearable.

Meanwhile, the carriage continued toward Melen.

None of the three girls knew that the person they wanted to see was already there.

He was currently arranging his supplies, completely unaware that his peaceful afternoon might soon become considerably more complicated.

And considering Bell's usual luck—

Perhaps it was better that he didn't know.

**End of Chapter Eighty-One.**
