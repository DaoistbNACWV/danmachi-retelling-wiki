# DANMACHI: RETELLING OF THE LEGEND

## Chapter Eighty-Four — The Break

The Leviathan seal was intact.

That was the conclusion Riveria reached after the Loki Familia completed its inspection of the designated site. There were no visible signs of damage, no suspicious disturbance around the structure, and no indication that the seal had been tampered with.

It was, by every reasonable measure, good news.

It also meant that the expedition could finally relax.

Or, more accurately, most of the expedition could.

"Then we're finished?" one of the girls asked.

"For the moment," Riveria replied. "We will remain in Melen until our departure. Keep your belongings organized, and do not wander somewhere dangerous."

A chorus of cheerful acknowledgements followed.

Riveria watched them disperse with the resigned expression of someone who already knew that at least half of those instructions would be ignored.

Ais, Alicia, and Lefiya were among the first to leave.

They had no particular destination in mind. The inspection was over, the weather was pleasant, and the coastline offered a welcome change from the familiar streets of Orario.

Still, their conversation kept circling back to the same person.

"Do you think Bell would like this place?" Alicia asked.

Ais looked toward the sea.

"Probably."

Lefiya glanced at her.

"Why are you answering so quickly?"

"Because he likes the sea."

"That doesn't mean he would like every beach!"

Alicia smiled.

"Perhaps we should ask him."

Lefiya's expression changed.

"He's not here."

"Isn't he?"

"Well, I don't know where he is, but he certainly isn't accompanying us."

Ais remained quiet.

The three continued along the beach, following the curve of the coastline. Their conversation gradually drifted elsewhere, though the thought of Bell never quite disappeared.

Then Ais noticed something.

A thin trail of smoke rose from a patch of elevated ground beyond the beach.

She stopped.

"What is it?" Alicia asked.

Ais pointed.

"Smoke."

Lefiya followed her gaze.

"Someone's camping?"

"Perhaps," Alicia said.

The location was somewhat secluded, separated from the busier stretch of beach by vegetation and a gentle rise in the terrain.

Ais studied it for a moment.

"Let's look."

Lefiya blinked.

"Why?"

"To see who's there."

"That is not a particularly convincing reason!"

Alicia was already walking.

"Come along, Lefiya."

The elf sighed and followed.

They took a narrow path through the vegetation. The sound of the waves gradually softened behind them, replaced by the rustling of leaves and the occasional cry of a distant seabird.

As they approached the campsite, voices became audible.

One belonged to a woman.

The other—

Lefiya stopped.

She knew that voice.

Ais stepped forward.

And then all three saw them.

Bell was sitting beneath the shade of a makeshift shelter, comfortably leaning against a rolled blanket. Filvis was beside him, nestled against his chest with one arm resting across his waist.

His other arm was wrapped around her.

They were cuddling.

Not particularly concerned with the outside world, either.

Filvis was speaking quietly, her head resting beneath Bell's chin. He occasionally answered, though his attention seemed to be divided between the conversation and the peaceful scenery beyond the campsite.

It was an ordinary, affectionate moment.

A private one.

And the three visitors had walked directly into it.

For several seconds, nobody moved.

Ais stared.

Lefiya's mouth opened, but no sound emerged.

Alicia blinked once.

Then she smiled.

"...Oh."

Bell looked toward the entrance.

His expression barely changed.

"Hello."

That was it.

Just hello.

He didn't jump away from Filvis. He didn't hurriedly remove his arm. He didn't even look particularly embarrassed.

He simply greeted them as though they had arrived at an entirely ordinary time.

Filvis raised her head.

She noticed the three girls.

Her eyes widened slightly.

"...Oh."

Unlike Bell, she seemed to need a moment to process the situation.

Ais continued staring.

"Bell."

"Yes?"

"What are you doing?"

Bell glanced down at Filvis, then back at her.

"Cuddling."

Lefiya finally found her voice.

"Y-You can't just say that so casually!"

Bell tilted his head.

"Would you prefer a more complicated explanation?"

"THAT IS NOT WHAT I MEANT!"

Alicia covered her mouth, though her shoulders were already shaking.

Filvis's cheeks had turned faintly pink. She shifted as if considering sitting upright, but Bell's arm remained comfortably around her.

He looked at her.

"Are you comfortable?"

Filvis hesitated.

Then she nodded.

"Yes."

"All right."

And he returned his attention to the visitors.

Ais looked between them.

Then at Bell's arm.

Then at Filvis.

Her expression was difficult to interpret.

"...You're close."

Filvis smiled a little.

"We are."

Lefiya looked as though she had just witnessed something personally offensive.

"Since when?!"

Bell considered the question.

"Since a while ago."

"THAT DOESN'T ANSWER ANYTHING!"

"It answers the question of when."

Alicia finally laughed.

"Bell, you haven't changed at all."

"I'd hope not."

"Even when you're caught doing something like this?"

Bell glanced at Filvis again.

"Is there something wrong with it?"

Alicia's smile softened.

"No. Not at all."

That seemed to settle the matter for him.

Lefiya, however, was not finished.

"Then why didn't you tell us you were in Melen?!"

"I didn't know you were coming."

"That's—"

She stopped.

That was, unfortunately, a reasonable answer.

Ais took another step closer.

"How long have you been here?"

"Since yesterday."

"With Filvis?"

"Yes."

Ais nodded slowly.

Her gaze returned to the two of them.

Bell noticed.

"Do you want to sit down?"

Ais blinked.

"...Can I?"

"Of course."

He gestured toward the spare seating near the table.

Alicia accepted immediately.

Lefiya remained standing, still struggling to reconcile the scene with the image she had apparently carried in her head.

Filvis looked at her.

"Would you like something to drink?"

Lefiya straightened.

"N-No, thank you!"

"There's enough for everyone."

"...Then perhaps a little."

Bell reached toward the supplies.

Alicia watched him with an amused expression.

"You really are relaxed."

"Should I be worried?"

"Three girls unexpectedly discover you cuddling with your girlfriend, and you greet us like visitors arriving for tea."

Bell paused.

Then nodded.

"That is more or less what happened."

Alicia laughed again.

Filvis lowered her face against his shoulder, hiding a small smile.

Lefiya looked at the sky.

She had come to Melen hoping for a pleasant vacation.

She had not expected to find Bell.

She certainly had not expected to find him like this.

And judging by his complete lack of embarrassment, he had no intention of making the situation easier for her.

Ais eventually sat down.

She remained quiet for a moment before asking the question that had been on her mind since she first saw them.

"Bell."

"Hm?"

"Can we stay for a while?"

Bell smiled.

"Of course."

Filvis nodded in agreement.

The three girls had discovered them by accident.

Their peaceful afternoon was over.

Bell, however, didn't seem to mind.

He merely adjusted the blanket around Filvis's shoulders and continued holding her.

The break, it seemed, had become considerably more crowded.

**End of Chapter Eighty-Four.**
