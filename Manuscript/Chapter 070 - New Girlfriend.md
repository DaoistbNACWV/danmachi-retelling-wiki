# Chapter Seventy — New Girlfriend

## Dionysus POV

"What the fuck do you mean she failed?!"

The shout echoed throughout the chamber.

Dionysus stood behind his desk, his usually composed expression twisted into something far less pleasant. His fingers dug into the edge of the furniture as he stared at the woman standing before him.

"After everything we've prepared? All those arrangements, all those sacrifices, and you come back telling me that the entire thing failed?!"

He abruptly swept his arm across the desk.

Several documents, glass containers, and expensive ornaments crashed onto the floor. One particularly unfortunate vase shattered against the wall.

"I specifically instructed both of you to fuse! Do you understand what that means? Both of you! Together! How difficult could that possibly have been?!"

Filvis remained silent.

Dionysus paced around the room, running a hand through his hair.

"How? How did this happen?"

He stopped in front of her.

"Filvis."

His voice became quieter.

"Why didn't you convince her? You were supposed to make her understand. You knew how important this was. Why didn't you convince her to cooperate?"

Filvis lowered her eyes.

"I apologize, my lord."

"That's not an answer!"

He struck the desk with his palm.

For a moment, the room fell silent.

Then Dionysus approached her again. This time, the anger in his expression had given way to something considerably more disturbing.

Desperation.

"Tell me something."

He placed both hands on her shoulders.

"Can you still feel her?"

Filvis looked up.

"Can you still sense her presence? The connection between you two? Is it still there?"

His fingers tightened.

"ANSWER ME!"

The goddess's voice cracked.

"Do you still have a connection?!"

Filvis hesitated before nodding.

Dionysus released a trembling breath.

"Good..."

He stepped backward, covering his face.

"Good. Then there's still a chance."

He began pacing again, muttering something under his breath. His thoughts were moving faster than he could organize them.

The plan had been simple.

Two halves. One purpose.

A reunion that would produce something far more useful than either individual could ever become alone.

And yet, somehow, it had failed.

One had refused.

The other had slipped beyond the intended arrangement.

Everything was becoming increasingly difficult to control.

Dionysus stopped.

"Listen carefully, Filvis."

His expression turned serious.

"If she has escaped your grasp, I want you to tell me immediately. Don't attempt anything reckless. Don't approach her without understanding the situation."

He paused.

"And please..."

His voice softened.

"Don't go outside without a disguise. Not now. Not when everything is so close to falling apart."

Filvis nodded.

"I understand, my lord."

"I don't want everything to collapse because of one mistake."

He looked toward the broken ornaments scattered across the floor.

"After all this time..."

His expression darkened.

"I refuse to lose everything now."

Filvis bowed before turning toward the door.

"Then I'll take my leave."

"Yes. Go."

The door closed behind her.

Dionysus remained standing in the middle of his chamber.

For several seconds, he simply stared at the empty doorway.

Then he whispered.

"Where are you?"

He wasn't asking Filvis.

He was asking the other one.

The one who had refused to cooperate.

The one who had somehow become the missing piece in a plan that should never have been allowed to fail.

And somewhere beyond the walls of his mansion, that missing piece was already living a life he had never intended for her.

## Filvis POV

The corridor was considerably quieter than the chamber she had just left.

Filvis walked at a measured pace, her footsteps barely audible against the polished floor.

She didn't look back.

Not even once.

Only after reaching a secluded section of the corridor did she finally stop.

Her expression changed.

The obedient smile disappeared, replaced by something considerably more genuine.

She placed a hand against her chest.

"My former Familia..."

Her eyes lowered.

"By my former Familia, I will never forget everything you've done for me, my lord."

For a moment, she closed her eyes.

Memories surfaced.

The people who had accepted her. The kindness she had received. The days when she had been something other than a tool in someone else's plans.

Astrea.

Her former goddess.

The one whose kindness she had never truly forgotten.

"May Astrea-sama forgive me."

She opened her eyes.

There was no hesitation in them now.

She had spent enough time pretending.

Enough time following instructions.

Enough time allowing someone else's ambitions to dictate what she should become.

Dionysus believed that she was still his obedient subordinate.

He believed that she would continue following his orders.

And, most importantly, he believed that the connection between the two Filvises remained something he could exploit.

She allowed him to believe that.

It was necessary.

A small lie could sometimes accomplish more than a thousand confrontations.

Her gaze shifted toward the window.

Beyond it lay the streets of Orario.

Somewhere in that city was the person who had changed everything.

And beside him was the other Filvis.

The one Dionysus wanted so desperately to reclaim.

Filvis resumed walking.

There were arrangements to make, information to gather, and more important matters that required her attention.

She had already wasted enough time serving someone else's purpose.

This time, she would choose what to do with her remaining life.

Even if it meant standing against the very person who had given her a second chance.

## Bell POV

"Ah, life."

Bell leaned against the balcony railing, enjoying the afternoon breeze.

He looked toward the courtyard, where the sunlight illuminated the small garden surrounding the mansion.

"Now this place feels considerably more alive."

He smiled.

"We're four people now. That's amazing."

He paused.

"Well, technically, that's not counting the occasional visitors."

He chuckled to himself.

Hestia.

Haruhime.

And now Filvis.

Who would've thought?

A few months ago, the idea of having this many people under one roof would have seemed rather amusing.

Now it was becoming a reality.

The mansion was no longer quite as quiet as it used to be. There were conversations, footsteps, occasional arguments, and the wonderful smell of freshly prepared food.

Speaking of which...

Bell glanced toward the kitchen.

Haruhime was undoubtedly preparing something again.

The girl was surprisingly talented.

Her cooking had improved considerably, and she seemed genuinely happy whenever someone complimented her meals.

There was, however, one particular issue.

They hadn't registered her with the Guild as a member of the Familia.

It wasn't because she wasn't welcome.

Quite the opposite.

Her abilities and magic were simply too valuable to casually expose through official records. There were things better kept away from the public eye, especially when certain people might become interested in acquiring them.

Bell preferred to avoid unnecessary attention.

They already had enough problems.

And now there was Filvis.

He leaned back against the railing.

"Well, this certainly complicates things."

His expression became thoughtful.

The situation surrounding Filvis was considerably more complicated than it appeared.

He had read the available reports concerning the failed expedition of the Astrea Familia.

An expedition that had ended in tragedy.

An enemy Familia had trapped them, leaving only two survivors: Ryuu and Filvis.

At least, that was what the reports indicated.

The surviving Filvis had eventually been transferred to Dionysus for reasons that weren't particularly clear.

Or, more accurately, the explanation provided to the public wasn't particularly convincing.

Bell had his own suspicions.

Dionysus was a dangerous individual.

Not necessarily because of his physical strength, but because of the information he possessed and the things he was capable of arranging behind the scenes.

He knew too much.

And there was something else.

The Dungeon.

Bell's eyes narrowed slightly.

He knew that Dionysus had some form of connection to it.

The exact nature of that connection remained uncertain, but the possibility alone was enough to warrant caution.

A god who could communicate with something inside the Dungeon was not someone Bell wanted to underestimate.

And then there was the matter of the two Filvises.

He looked toward the sky.

One was here.

The other was with Dionysus.

They shared the same identity, appearance, and history to a certain extent, but they were not the same individual.

They were separate entities.

That distinction was important.

Very important.

Whatever Dionysus had intended to accomplish by bringing them together, it hadn't happened.

And judging by the circumstances, he wasn't particularly pleased about it.

Bell rubbed his chin.

"So, what should I do?"

He already had an answer.

Actually, he had several.

The first was obvious.

Filvis/Ein needed to become an official member of the Hestia Familia.

Not simply someone living under their roof, but someone whose presence could be publicly justified.

That would make it considerably more difficult for Dionysus to approach her without attracting attention.

It would also establish a clear distinction between her current life and whatever arrangements had previously been made for her.

The second was somewhat more amusing.

He would announce that they were in a relationship.

Bell blinked.

Then smiled.

It wasn't an entirely fabricated arrangement.

There was already a certain degree of affection between them, although the circumstances surrounding it were admittedly unusual.

Besides, if people believed that Filvis was his girlfriend, fewer questions would be raised about why she was staying with him.

At least, that was the theory.

There would undoubtedly be questions.

Especially from Hestia.

Bell could already imagine the reaction.

He sighed.

"Well, I'll deal with that when it happens."

There was one final matter.

The most important one.

Filvis/Ein's condition.

He looked down at his hand.

The Dungeon had done something to her.

Calling her a revived person would be an oversimplification. The process had produced something that could be described as a creature, an existence that had been brought back through a method that was anything but natural.

The implications were considerable.

He didn't particularly like the idea of leaving her in that condition.

He wanted to understand what had happened to her body, identify the changes, and determine whether they could be reversed or neutralized.

Not because he disliked what she had become.

But because she deserved to have a choice.

If she wanted to live as an ordinary person, then he would help her pursue that possibility.

If she wanted something different, they would discuss it.

The important thing was that the decision belonged to her.

Bell exhaled.

"First things first."

He straightened.

"Let's get her settled into the Familia."

And then, perhaps, he could finally enjoy a relatively peaceful afternoon.

A rather optimistic expectation, considering his current circumstances.

But one could always dream.

"Bell?"

He turned.

Filvis/Ein stood near the balcony entrance.

Her silver hair moved slightly in the breeze, and her expression carried a hint of uncertainty.

"Oh, there you are."

Bell smiled.

"Is something wrong?"

She shook her head.

"Not particularly."

"Then?"

She approached him.

"I heard you talking to yourself."

Bell blinked.

"Ah."

He scratched his cheek.

"Sorry. I tend to do that sometimes."

"I noticed."

"Is that so?"

"Yes."

She looked toward the garden.

Then back at him.

"May I ask you something?"

"Of course."

Bell waited.

Filvis hesitated for a moment.

Then she spoke.

"About what you said earlier..."

"Earlier?"

"You mentioned that I should become part of your Familia."

Bell nodded.

"Yes. I was planning to discuss it with Hestia. If you're comfortable with the idea, I'd like to have you officially join us."

She looked at him.

"Why?"

Bell tilted his head.

"Why?"

"Yes. Why would you go through all that trouble for me?"

He considered the question.

There were many possible answers.

He could mention her condition, her circumstances, or the danger posed by Dionysus.

But none of those quite captured what he wanted to say.

"Because I want you to have somewhere you can call home."

Filvis went still.

Bell continued.

"You don't have to repay me for saving you. You don't have to prove that you're useful, either. If you want to stay, then stay. If you want to leave someday, that's your decision."

He smiled.

"That's what being part of a Familia should mean, right?"

She lowered her eyes.

"...I see."

For a brief moment, neither spoke.

Then Bell remembered something.

"Oh, and there's one more thing I'd like to discuss."

Filvis looked up.

"What is it?"

Bell gave her a slightly awkward smile.

"Well, there's the matter of us announcing our relationship."

Silence.

A very particular kind of silence.

Filvis stared at him.

"...Our relationship?"

"Yes."

"Which relationship?"

Bell blinked.

"Uh..."

He scratched his cheek.

"Well, I was thinking that we could tell everyone we're dating."

Her eyes widened.

"Dating?"

"Yes."

"Us?"

"That's generally how it works."

Bell smiled awkwardly.

Filvis continued staring.

Then her expression changed.

Her cheeks gradually became pink.

"...I don't mind."

Bell blinked.

"Really?"

She nodded.

"Yes."

He looked somewhat surprised.

"That's good. I was worried you might find the suggestion uncomfortable."

Filvis looked away.

"No. I don't mind at all."

Bell smiled.

"Then it's settled."

He turned toward the mansion.

"Let's go talk to Hestia."

Filvis remained in place for another moment.

Then she followed him.

Her heart was beating considerably faster than it should have.

And she was beginning to realize that perhaps the situation was much more complicated than she had initially expected.
