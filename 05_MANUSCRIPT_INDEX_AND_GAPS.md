# DANMACHI: RETELLING OF THE LEGEND

## Manuscript Index, Completeness Audit, and Recovery Instructions

## Purpose

This document prevents a continuity pack from being mistaken for the
actual novel. It records what was and was not retrievable when this pack
was assembled.

## Critical distinction

-   A **chapter summary** is not a finalized chapter.
-   A **portable continuity command** is not a manuscript.
-   A **master continuity file** is not a substitute for dialogue,
    scenes, prose, or chapter endings.
-   Do not claim that the full Arc 1--8 manuscript is contained in this
    pack.

## What was retrievable

-   DanMachi portable reference command.
-   Master Continuity Filter / Primary Command.
-   Arc 2 portable handoff.
-   Arc 3 story checkpoint/save with Chapters 13--16 summarized.
-   Arc 4 portable continuity command.
-   Arc 6 portable handoff, which leaves Arc 5/6 specifics open.
-   Arc 8 portable continuity command and a later Arc 8 active character
    roster lock.
-   User-established writing workflow and project context.

## What was not verified as complete

-   Full finalized chapter prose for Arcs 1--8.
-   A complete master manuscript containing all finalized chapters.
-   Complete Arc 1, Arc 2, Arc 4, Arc 5, Arc 6, and Arc 7
    chapter-by-chapter prose.
-   Full Master Character List / TRUE-FALSE file.
-   Complete Falna/status ledger and current Bell sheet.
-   Complete location, item, spell, skill, and relationship databases.

## Before calling this pack a complete manuscript

1.  Retrieve the finalized chapter files from the user's Project or
    Library.
2.  Include only user-finalized/approved chapter versions.
3.  Preserve chapter numbering, titles, formatting, and endings.
4.  Resolve duplicate versions by checking timestamps and user approval,
    not by choosing the newest file blindly.
5.  Keep drafts separate from finalized chapters.
6.  Add a table of contents and arc separators.
7.  Run a duplicate/missing-chapter audit.
8.  Only then label the compilation
    `MASTER MANUSCRIPT — FINALIZED CHAPTERS`.

## Safe use tonight

The five reference files in this pack can be uploaded and used as a
**continuity/reference layer** immediately. They are not a replacement
for the full finalized chapter archive. The most important immediate
benefit is that they tell a writing assistant how to handle continuity,
what is confirmed, what must remain unknown, and which Arc 8 characters
are prohibited.

## Recommended six Project slots

1.  `01_PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`
2.  `02_MASTER_CONTINUITY.md`
3.  `03_ARC_CHECKPOINTS.md`
4.  `04_CHARACTER_AND_WORLD_REFERENCE.md`
5.  `05_MANUSCRIPT_INDEX_AND_GAPS.md`
6.  Reserve for the active arc's actual chapter bundle or latest source
    manuscript.

## Update rule

After every completed arc or major retcon: - Update
`02_MASTER_CONTINUITY.md`. - Update `03_ARC_CHECKPOINTS.md`. - Update
`04_CHARACTER_AND_WORLD_REFERENCE.md` only with supported new facts. -
Preserve older arc checkpoints as historical records. - Never silently
overwrite a user-approved finalized chapter with a draft.
