## 2. JJK's supernatural foundation

### 2.1 Cursed Energy

Cursed Energy (呪力, Juryoku) is generated by negative emotions experienced by humans. Ordinary humans generally leak cursed energy without controlling it. Accumulations of leaked energy can give rise to cursed spirits. Jujutsu sorcerers learn to control and direct cursed energy.

Negative emotions commonly associated with cursed energy include fear, anger, grief, hatred, jealousy, and regret. This is a broad description, not a rule that every instance of an emotion creates a spirit.

Important distinctions:
- **Cursed energy:** The energy resource.
- **Cursed technique:** A supernatural technique that uses cursed energy and is commonly innate.
- **Cursed energy reinforcement:** Applying cursed energy to the body or an object to improve offensive or defensive performance.
- **Cursed energy output:** The amount of energy that can be released at a given moment.
- **Efficiency/control:** How economically and precisely a user manipulates energy.
- **Cursed energy reserves:** The amount available to the user.

High reserves do not automatically equal high skill, output, efficiency, tactical ability, or victory.

### 2.2 Cursed techniques

Many sorcerers possess an innate cursed technique engraved into their body/brain. Techniques have individual rules, conditions, limitations, and possible applications. They are not generally interchangeable skills that anyone can learn by watching.

Useful categories and terms:
- **Innate technique:** A user's inborn technique.
- **Inherited technique:** A technique transmitted through a family line.
- **Extension technique:** An application developed from an existing technique.
- **Maximum technique:** An exceptionally powerful expression of a technique, distinct from Domain Expansion.
- **Technique reversal:** Reversing an innate technique's effect by applying positive energy, where the technique supports it.
- **Shikigami:** Summoned entities used through jujutsu techniques.
- **Barrier techniques:** Techniques used to construct or manipulate barriers and enclosed spaces.

Examples:
- Gojo Satoru — Limitless, a space-related inherited technique.
- Megumi Fushiguro — Ten Shadows Technique, involving shikigami.
- Nobara Kugisaki — Straw Doll Technique, using a sympathetic link to attack a target.
- Yuta Okkotsu — Copy, a special technique with conditions and limitations; it is not proof that copying is generally available.
- Mahito — Idle Transfiguration, which manipulates the shape of the soul.

### 2.3 Domain Expansion

Domain Expansion (領域展開) manifests a user's innate domain, generally through a barrier, and imbues it with a cursed technique. A completed conventional domain commonly grants a sure-hit effect to its imbued technique. Domains can vary substantially in construction and rules.

Examples:
- Gojo — Unlimited Void.
- Sukuna — Malevolent Shrine, notable for its open-barrier construction.
- Mahito — Self-Embodiment of Perfection.
- Hakari — Idle Death Gamble.

Domain-related counters and defenses include:
- Another Domain Expansion, producing a domain clash.
- Simple Domain.
- Hollow Wicker Basket.
- Falling Blossom Emotion.
- Domain Amplification.

These defenses do not all do the same thing and should not be treated as universal counters. Domain clashes involve refinement, barrier conditions, and technique interactions—not simply a comparison of total energy reserves. Domain Expansion also commonly causes technique burnout or temporary impairment afterward.

**Crossover caution:** Do not assume Bell's Dispel automatically cancels a Domain Expansion. A domain involves several interacting components: a barrier, an innate domain, an imbued technique, and often a sure-hit rule. Any Bell counter must be established by identifying what he can perceive and affect.

### 2.4 Reverse Cursed Technique and positive energy

Reverse Cursed Technique (RCT) is the process of producing positive energy from cursed energy, commonly by multiplying negative energy by itself. It is difficult to perform. It can heal biological bodies, and positive energy can be harmful to cursed spirits. RCT is not the same thing as ordinary healing or a general-purpose reversal of any supernatural effect.

Do not assume that Bell's healing magic, mana manipulation, or other restorative abilities are RCT. Their compatibility is a crossover question.

### 2.5 Binding vows

Binding vows exchange restrictions or risks for benefits. They can be made with oneself or with others, with different rules and consequences. Revealing information, accepting limitations, or imposing conditions can alter a technique's effectiveness.

A binding vow is not a free wish or unrestricted power multiplier. Its benefit, cost, enforceability, and consequences must be considered. Do not invent a vow that grants an arbitrary result without a meaningful restriction.

### 2.6 Heavenly Restriction

Heavenly Restriction is a condition imposed from birth that exchanges one aspect of a person's body or cursed-energy potential for another. It is not an ordinary voluntary vow.

Examples:
- Toji Fushiguro: Extremely unusual physical abilities in exchange for effectively no cursed energy.
- Kokichi Muta (Mechamaru): Severe bodily limitations associated with exceptional cursed-energy range and quantity.
- Maki Zenin: Her later state demonstrates the extreme physical outcome associated with the absence of cursed energy.

This establishes that JJK recognizes unusual trade-offs between body, cursed energy, and physical capability. It does not establish that every foreign power system is a form of Heavenly Restriction.

## 3. Cursed spirits and exorcism

Cursed spirits are entities generally formed from accumulated cursed energy. They range from relatively unintelligent threats to highly intelligent beings capable of speech, planning, alliances, and innate techniques. Some can use barriers or Domain Expansion.

Sorcerers classify curses and sorcerers through grades:
- Grade 4
- Grade 3
- Grade 2
- Semi-Grade 1
- Grade 1
- Special Grade

The grade system is an administrative and threat-classification framework, not a perfectly precise universal combat scale. Special Grade covers exceptional cases and does not mean every Special Grade has identical strength or abilities. The often-cited nation-overthrow benchmark is a broad description of Special Grade sorcerer potential, not a guarantee against every possible opponent or military.

### Exorcism rule

Ordinarily, cursed spirits must be exorcised using cursed energy. Cursed tools and other JJK-specific interactions can also matter. A mundane physical attack is not automatically sufficient to permanently exorcise a curse.

**Critical crossover question — unresolved:** Can Bell's mana or magic perceive, touch, injure, suppress, or permanently destroy cursed spirits?

Do not silently choose an answer. Test the following separately:
1. Detection/perception.
2. Physical contact.
3. Damage to the spirit's manifested form.
4. Disruption of its cursed-energy structure.
5. Permanent exorcism.
6. Interaction with a curse's innate technique or domain.

Possible crossover models (not yet approved):
- **Strict separation:** Mana can interact physically but cannot exorcise without a discovered conversion or special condition.
- **Partial compatibility:** Mana can damage curses, but permanent exorcism still requires a specific mechanism.
- **Functional compatibility:** Mana can serve as an effective exorcising energy, while remaining metaphysically distinct from cursed energy.

Do not select a model merely to make Bell stronger or weaker. Choose the model that best supports the intended story and remains consistent.

## 4. Soul, body, and identity

JJK gives the soul and body substantial metaphysical importance.

Relevant canon examples:
- **Mahito / Idle Transfiguration:** Mahito perceives and alters the shape of the soul; bodily form is affected by the soul's shape.
- **Yuji and Sukuna:** Their coexistence demonstrates that distinct souls/persons can inhabit one body under unusual circumstances.

These mechanics do not establish that all souls are identical, that every soul can be manipulated in the same way, or that foreign entities automatically obey JJK's metaphysics.

- Can JJK soul techniques affect Bell? If so, to what extent?

**Do not assume:** Bell is immune to Idle Transfiguration because he is associated with Chronos. Any resistance or vulnerability must be grounded in established Bell lore and demonstrated mechanics.

## 5. Jujutsu society and infrastructure

JJK's supernatural society operates largely behind ordinary human society.

Important institutions and roles:
- **Tokyo Jujutsu High:** Trains and organizes sorcerers.
- **Kyoto Jujutsu High:** The other major school.
- **Jujutsu Headquarters:** Central administrative and political authority.
- **Big Three Families:** Major hereditary sorcerer clans with political and technical influence.
- **Curse users:** Humans who use jujutsu for criminal or hostile purposes.
- **Windows:** Non-sorcerers who can perceive curses to varying degrees and support jujutsu operations.
- **Auxiliary Managers:** Provide logistical support, transport, curtains, and mission coordination.
- **Tengen:** A central figure in Japan's barrier infrastructure.

### Tengen and barriers

Tengen's barrier techniques are foundational to the structure of Japanese jujutsu society and are connected to the unusual concentration and refinement of cursed energy in Japan. Tengen's evolution and relationship with Kenjaku are central to the Culling Game and merger plot.

Do not reduce Tengen's barriers to ordinary physical walls. Barrier techniques can impose conditions and shape supernatural spaces.

## 7. Combat and power-scaling framework

JJK combat cannot be ranked by destructive output alone. Consider:
1. Cursed-energy reserves.
2. Output.
3. Efficiency and control.
4. Innate technique and its conditions.
5. Physical speed, strength, durability, and senses.
6. RCT and survivability.
7. Barrier and Domain proficiency.
8. Tactical intelligence and experience.
9. Matchup-specific interactions.
10. Binding vows, tools, and environmental conditions.

A rough descriptive ladder:
- Grade 4–3: Lower-grade sorcerers and curses.
- Grade 2: Competent field combatants.
- Grade 1: Elite, highly capable sorcerers.
- Special Grade: Exceptional individuals or threats with unusual potential.
- Top-end exceptional cases: Gojo and Sukuna, whose abilities and matchups place them beyond ordinary comparisons.

This is a qualitative guide, not a mathematical conversion chart. Individual matchups can overturn broad rankings.

### Combat mechanics worth tracking
- **Black Flash:** A rare phenomenon when cursed energy is applied within an extremely short interval of a physical impact, dramatically increasing the strike's effect. It is not a button a fighter can activate at will.
- **RCT:** Positive-energy production used for healing and certain other applications.
- **Maximum techniques:** Extreme applications of certain innate techniques.
- **Domain clashes:** Governed by domain refinement, barrier construction, and conditions.
- **Technique burnout:** A common consequence after Domain Expansion.
- **Heavenly Restriction:** Can produce unusual physical or cursed-energy profiles.
- **Cursed tools:** Weapons imbued with cursed energy or possessing special properties.

Do not convert DanMachi Levels, Falna stats, or Bell's numerical values directly into JJK grades. The systems measure different things.

## 9. Bell-specific crossover principles

These are proposed working principles pending author approval:

1. **Keep mana and cursed energy distinct by default.** Similar narrative functions do not prove identical metaphysics.
2. **Falna is not automatically recognized by JJK.** JJK characters should not know what Falna, Status, Levels, Development Abilities, or a Familia are without investigation.
3. **Do not automatically translate Bell's abilities into cursed techniques.** A foreign ability remains foreign unless a specific interaction justifies reclassification.
4. **Mushin is not unrestricted copying.** Bell's established concept is the absence of rejection: he observes a technique, understands it, and refines it. This does not automatically grant biological traits, cursed-energy prerequisites, inherited techniques, or domain proficiency.
5. **Dispel is not an automatic answer to every JJK ability.** Determine what it can detect and affect in each case.
6. **Bell's Chronos identity is not an automatic victory condition.** His incomplete self-understanding and existing restrictions remain relevant.
7. **Do not activate God Mode.** The author's explicit activation phrase remains required.
8. **Preserve uncertainty.** JJK characters should form hypotheses from evidence rather than instantly understand Bell.
9. **Preserve JJK limitations.** Domain rules, binding vows, technique conditions, RCT limitations, and cursed-spirit mechanics must remain meaningful.
10. **Avoid automatic power conversion.** No direct equation between DanMachi Level, Falna stats, mana reserves, cursed-energy quantity, or JJK grades.

### Recommended narrative direction

JJK should initially function as a new supernatural research environment, not merely another arena for Bell to defeat increasingly powerful opponents. Bell has to learn how the world works, while the inhabitants have to interpret an existence that does not fit their categories.

The tension should come from incomplete information, incompatible assumptions, practical danger, and the consequences of experimentation.

## 12. Retelling Bell's JJK Reincarnation — Gojo Toki

**Status:** AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED CROSSOVER PREMISE  
**Character identity in JJK:** Gojo Toki  
**Origin:** Retelling Bell Cranel, whose deeper identity is Chronos  
**Birth:** Born into the Gojo Clan  
**Memory awakening:** Age five

### 12.1 Reincarnation and cleansing

After being expelled from Gekai, Retelling Bell undergoes a reset and is reborn into the Gojo Clan as **Gojo Toki**.

His soul is cleansed as part of this transition. However, the cleansing does not erase his memories: because he is also a god, he retains all of his memories despite the reset. Those memories return to conscious access when he reaches the age of awakening, **five years old**.

Keep these two facts distinct:
- **Soul cleansing/reset:** The reincarnation involves a cleansing of his soul. Do not assume that his former body's condition, Falna, or every prior metaphysical attachment transfers unchanged.
- **Divine memory retention:** His godhood allows him to retain his memories through the cleansing and reincarnation. At five, he regains access to those memories.

**Unresolved metaphysical detail:** The exact mechanism by which a cleansed soul retains memories because of its divine nature has not yet been specified. Do not invent a detailed explanation or contradict the premise. Treat memory retention as an established crossover fact and its underlying mechanism as awaiting further definition.

### 12.2 New identity and upbringing

Gojo Toki is born as a member of the Gojo Clan in the JJK world. He is not simply Bell wearing a new name or an adult mind placed into an existing body: the stated premise is a new birth and a new life, followed by the return of his retained memories at age five.

### 12.3 Innate Cursed Technique — Time Manipulation

Gojo Toki's innate cursed technique is **Time Manipulation**.

This is his JJK-native innate cursed technique. It should be treated as the foundation from which its named applications and future developments arise. Do not conflate it with Bell's former DanMachi magic or automatically classify every former ability as an application of this cursed technique.

### 12.4 Named application — Eternity

**Eternity** is a time-based defensive technique/application of Time Manipulation.

Its established effect is to impose **time negation** upon anything that approaches Gojo Toki. Unlike Gojo Satoru's Limitless technique, which manipulates the space between the user and an approaching object so that it cannot reach him in the ordinary way, Eternity literally cancels the incoming attack through temporal negation.

The author's stated scope is absolute in wording: it can cancel **any and all attacks** that approach him.

Important distinction:
- **Gojo Satoru — Infinity:** A spatial effect associated with Limitless that prevents an approaching object from reaching him by manipulating the space involved.
- **Gojo Toki — Eternity:** A temporal defensive effect that negates an approaching attack through time manipulation.

Do not describe Eternity as merely slowing attacks, making them infinitely distant, or functioning as a reskinned Infinity. Its defining principle is time negation and cancellation.

**Details not yet specified:** Whether Eternity is continuously active, what counts as an attack, how it identifies hostile intent, whether it affects indirect/environmental phenomena, its range, its interaction with domains and sure-hit effects, and whether it has any cost or limitation. Preserve the established claim that it cancels any and all approaching attacks; do not quietly add exceptions or weaknesses. Record future clarifications from the author.

### 12.5 Maximum Output — The World

**The World** is the Maximum Output of Gojo Toki's Time Manipulation.

Its defining effect is **Time Stop**: it stops time.

This is distinct from Eternity:
- **Eternity:** Defensive temporal negation of incoming attacks.
- **The World:** Maximum-output temporal control that stops time.

Do not invent duration, range, target restrictions, activation requirements, energy cost, cooldown, or what Gojo Toki can do while time is stopped. These remain open for future definition.

### 12.8 Eternity's cost exploit — Stale State

**Status:** AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED MECHANIC

Eternity is naturally a **Cursed-Energy-hungry technique**. Its defensive effect is powerful, but its ordinary operation would impose a substantial CE burden.

Bell has discovered an exploit in the way his own Time Manipulation operates: **he can bind himself to a stale state**.

In this state, time behaves differently for Bell. He anchors himself to a temporal state in which Eternity's defensive condition is already established. The exploit prevents the technique's ordinary processing from reaching the point at which it consumes Cursed Energy.

The practical result is that Bell can remain in this stale state indefinitely during battle or combat, keeping Eternity's protection in effect without paying its ordinary ongoing CE cost.

#### Temporal output displacement — the perpetual-motion analogy

The author's analogy is a perpetual-motion machine whose output is not the energy it would generate in the current second, but the output it will generate in the **next second**, delivered early.

Applied to Bell's technique:
- The technique's future execution/output is brought forward and made available in the present.
- Its ordinary present-time processing is bypassed or prevented from reaching the CE-consumption stage.
- Bell is therefore using an effect whose execution belongs to the future, while his own temporal reference is anchored to a stale, past state.
- He remains in the present, but acts through that stale temporal reference.

This is the central paradox of the exploit: **Bell is in the present, moving through a frozen world by acting from a stale/past temporal state while using the output of a technique that, in ordinary sequence, belongs to his future.**

This is why Bell can move when time is frozen around him. He is not simply resisting the freeze through speed, strength, or conventional immunity; his own temporal state and the temporal source of the technique's output do not follow the same ordinary progression as the frozen surroundings.

The perpetual-motion comparison is an explanatory analogy for the temporal ordering of output. Do not reinterpret it as literal creation of energy from nothing, infinite CE reserves, or a conventional perpetual-energy generator. The exact metaphysical accounting of the future output has not yet been specified.

#### Conceptual comparison

The closest comparison is Regulus Corneas's *Lion's Heart* from *Re:Zero*, specifically the idea of maintaining a state that is removed from ordinary temporal progression. However, this is only an analogy for understanding the concept; Eternity's mechanism is Bell's own Time Manipulation, not Regulus's Authority.

Unlike the five-second limitation associated with Lion's Heart, Bell's stale-state exploit has **no stated five-second limit**. Do not import Regulus's exact rules into Bell's technique.

#### The food analogy

Cursed Energy can be thought of as food consumed by the technique. Ordinarily, Eternity would proceed through its operation and consume that energy. Bell's exploit prevents the consumption process from being reached: he effectively interrupts the process before the technique gets to eat.

The future-output analogy adds another dimension: the technique's next-second output is already available now, so Bell does not need to wait for the ordinary present-time process to consume CE and produce that output.

This is **not** infinite CE, energy generation, or ordinary CE efficiency. Bell is exploiting the temporal order of the technique's processing, output, and energy expenditure.

#### Continuity distinctions

- Eternity remains naturally CE-costly; the exploit does not make its ordinary operation inherently cheap.
- The stale state is the exploit that bypasses the ordinary CE expenditure.
- Bell can maintain this stale state indefinitely during combat, as currently established by the author.
- The technique's future execution/output is made available in the present, while Bell acts through a stale/past temporal reference.
- Bell remains in the present; the stale state describes the temporal reference through which he acts, not a literal relocation of his body to an earlier date.
- This is how Bell can move and act while the surrounding world is frozen in time.
- The mechanism is temporal, arising from Bell's Time Manipulation; it is not a barrier, spatial defense, or conventional automatic energy-saving mode.
- Do not invent a five-second timeout, a routine CE drain, or a fixed duration.
- The exact metaphysical accounting of future output, and the full scope of what else is frozen or excluded from the stale state, remain unspecified. Do not infer answers without author direction.
- This exploit applies to Eternity as described. Do not automatically extend it to The World or every other application of Time Manipulation.
