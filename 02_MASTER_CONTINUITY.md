## Project premise and protagonist direction

-   The story is a DanMachi retelling with a deliberately altered
    timeline and original project material.
-   Bell's development begins at age 10 in the user's retelling
    foundation.
-   The early premise includes the Land of Dreams and a mysterious
    Being/assistant that cannot answer every question.
-   Bell develops mentally through dream-time and later experiments with
    a prototype wand made from Forest of Wishe wood.
-   The prototype wand's stated estimates: approximately +12% output,
    -5% mental consumption, and +40% effective range. Treat these as
    project-specific figures, not canon.
-   Bell's intended characterization: capable but not the strongest,
    intelligent but not the smartest, observant and calculating when
    appropriate, but still flawed and emotionally human.
-   Bell's development should be earned. Avoid effortless power
    escalation.
