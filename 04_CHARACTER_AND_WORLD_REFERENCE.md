## Bell Cranel

### Established project direction

-   Protagonist of the retelling.
-   In the retelling foundation, Bell begins at age 10.
-   His early story includes the Land of Dreams and a mysterious
    Being/assistant that cannot answer every question.
-   He is intended to be capable but not the strongest, intelligent but
    not the smartest, and flawed rather than perfect.
-   His thoughts may be analytical/calculating, especially when
    assessing opponents, risks, abilities, and compatibility.
-   He should retain believable emotions and limitations.
-   He uses unconventional problem-solving, magic, mobility, and
    improvised tactics.
-   He wants to develop his own path rather than passively rely on a
    powerful group.
