## Welcome

This wiki is the reader-friendly and writer-facing reference layer for *DanMachi: Retelling of the Legend*. It is designed to make established information discoverable without requiring a search through every chapter, continuity file, and arc handoff.

It is **not** a replacement for the manuscript, the master continuity files, or the author's decisions. The wiki summarizes and points back to those sources.

## Project identity

This is a **DanMachi retelling**, not a chapter-by-chapter reproduction of canon. It includes a deliberately altered timeline, original project material, and author-approved divergences. Canon information is useful only where it remains compatible with the retelling.

The protagonist's development begins at age ten in the retelling foundation. The early premise includes the Land of Dreams and a mysterious Being/assistant. These are project-specific elements, not claims about original DanMachi canon.

## Evidence comes before completeness

Every article must distinguish established facts from interpretation and missing information. Unknown details stay unknown. A blank field is better than an invented level, spell, relationship, date, motive, or outcome.

The wiki's labels and source rules are defined in the [Continuity and canon guide](Continuity-Guide.md).

## Source-of-truth rule

When a wiki summary and an authoritative project source disagree, **the authoritative source wins**. Follow the hierarchy in [01_PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md](../01_PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md). The current manuscript, author corrections, approved continuity records, and arc-specific source material take priority over this wiki.

## How this wiki is maintained

1. Verify the relevant manuscript or approved continuity source.
2. Add only facts supported by that source.
3. Mark draft-only or unresolved material clearly.
4. Add a source pointer to every substantive article.
5. Update affected cross-links and the editorial status page.
6. Never silently rewrite finalized prose or turn an assistant suggestion into canon.
