# World, Familias, and Locations

[← Wiki home](README.md)

This page records setting elements that appear in the currently available project references. It is not intended to be a complete world encyclopedia yet.

## Locations

### Orario

Orario is the city Bell reaches during the Arc 3 checkpoint. The city is the setting for the Labyrinth City sequence. Bell seeks lodging after arriving, and the guards recommend the Hostess of Fertility.

### The Dungeon

The Dungeon is central to the setting and to Bell's later investigations.

Chapter 75, “By My Words,” records Bell forming a preliminary theory that the Dungeon can corrupt individuals who die within its territory and that sustaining even one corrupted existence requires substantial energy. This is Bell's deduction from observations, not a fully established universal explanation. The exact mechanism, scope, limits, and exceptions remain [OPEN].

### Under Resort — Eighteenth Floor

Chapter 65's checkpoint identifies the eighteenth floor as the Under Resort. Bell reunites with Finn there after destroying Goliath on the seventeenth floor.

### Hostess of Fertility

A lodging/tavern/cafe location recommended to Bell by guards when he reaches Orario in the Arc 3 checkpoint. Other details should be taken from the actual chapters.

## Familias and organizations

### Loki Familia

The Loki Familia takes an interest in Bell during the Arc 3 sequence. Finn challenges Bell to a duel. Bell later declines the offer to join, citing concerns about compatibility and the path he wants to pursue.

### Hestia Familia

The Arc 7 Chapter 46 checkpoint identifies a church as the Hestia Familia's base. The church and Bell's laboratory are destroyed in a fire. The arsonist is unknown in that checkpoint.

### Freya Familia

Freya attempts to recruit Bell during their three-day journey toward Orario. Bell declines. Freya gives him 100,000 valis at the city gate. Do not infer further recruitment or relationship outcomes from this checkpoint alone.

### Guild

Bell visits the Guild after the church fire to complain. The Arc 7 checkpoint says the Guild does not meaningfully respond. Bell suspects bribery, but bribery is not confirmed fact.

## Faction and location rules

- Distinguish an organization's established action from a character's suspicion about it.
- Do not treat rumors implicating the Apollo Familia in the church fire as proof of responsibility.
- Do not invent a Familia roster, rank, political position, or alliance to fill a gap.
- Check arc-specific character restrictions before writing or adding appearances.
