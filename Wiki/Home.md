## Authority and evidence

The wiki is a **derived reference**, not a source of canon. The latest explicit author decision, approved manuscript, and authoritative continuity records take precedence. Unknown facts remain `[OPEN]`; proposals remain `[OPTION]`; draft status is not approval.
