# Systems, Falna, Magic, and Equipment

[← Wiki home](README.md)

This page is an index of system rules and recorded project mechanics. It deliberately separates DanMachi canon concepts from original retelling mechanics.

## Falna and status

Falna, Level, attributes, skills, spells, and status sheets are continuity-sensitive. Never fill a missing value by inference.

- At the Arc 3 Chapter 15 checkpoint, Bell has no Falna.
- By Arc 4, Bell has a Familia and Falna.
- Exact Familia name, Level, attributes, skills, spells, and later changes must come from the latest applicable chapter or approved status save.
- A status sheet is time-specific. It must include a chapter/date context where available.

### Status sheet fields

Use the project's established fields when a status sheet is appropriate:

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Name / Age / Race | Record only if established. |
| Level | Record the level at the specific story point. |
| STR / END / DEX / AGI / MAG | Do not invent missing values. |
| Skills / Spells | Preserve established names and exact mechanics. |
| Familia | Record only when established at that point. |

## Magic and combat

### Arc 3 checkpoint

Bell's supported combat elements in the Arc 3 duel include:

- Magic barrier.
- Magic Missile.
- Explosions used for movement, distance, and disruption.
- Improvised concussive grenades.
- Mobility and unconventional tactics.

This is not a complete inventory of Bell's later abilities.

### Fluctuation and Arcanum

**[AUTHOR-CONFIRMED MECHANIC]** Bell accesses/employs Arcanum by filtering the Fluctuation.

Preserve the distinction:

- **Fluctuation** is the medium Bell filters.
- **Arcanum** is what he accesses/employs through that filtering.

Do not describe Fluctuation as simply identical to raw Arcanum. Chapter 74 records Bell's theory that Fluctuation is a residual manifestation of Arcanum, then frames Test Two as a controlled, direct Arcanum purification requiring divine authorization. The author-confirmed filtering mechanic must be preserved, but Chapters 74–75 do not fully narrate how that filtering interfaces with the planned direct application. The precise operational sequence, costs, limits, and role of the ritual inscriptions or Sumerian chant remain [OPEN]. Do not claim that the inscriptions or chant are the filtering mechanism.

### Resurrection incantation

The master continuity records a locked sequence:

- Bell used the resurrection incantation twice before the Karina scene.
- The earlier uses were for Bell's mother and aunt.
- The Karina scene is the third use.
- The incantation calls to the Underworld through an unknown medium.
- Hades hears the call and recognizes Bell.
- The Underworld is chaotic; do not assume Hades has total control over every event there.

Mechanics, costs, limits, and any unrecorded outcomes remain [OPEN]. Consult the actual chapter for scene detail.

## Equipment

### Forest of Wishe prototype wand

An early project-specific prototype is recorded with the following estimates:

| Property | Recorded estimate |
|---|---:|
| Output | +12% |
| Mental consumption | -5% |
| Effective range | +40% |

These values are estimates in the retelling's project material, not DanMachi canon. Bell tests a Mana Bullet with the prototype. Later modifications and exact mechanics remain [OPEN] unless supported by chapters.

### Crimson Order

Chapter 67's checkpoint identifies Crimson Order as a rapier originally forged as a gift for Astrea, who declined it. The recorded construction is primarily synthetic mithril with orichalcum components. Consult the chapter for its exact use and context.
