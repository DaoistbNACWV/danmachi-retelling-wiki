# Continuity, Canon, and Evidence Guide

[← Wiki home](README.md)

This page defines how the wiki distinguishes facts, retelling changes, proposals, and unresolved material.

## 1. Source-of-truth hierarchy

When sources disagree, use the project's established order:

1. Latest explicit author correction or decision.
2. Latest author-supplied chapter or draft for the active scene.
3. Latest confirmed master continuity save or character/event/timeline ledger.
4. Latest arc-specific save or approved checkpoint.
5. Earlier project saves and drafts.
6. DanMachi canon/reference material, only where compatible with the retelling.
7. Assistant-generated suggestions, which remain proposals until accepted.

The wiki is a **derived reference**, not an authority above these sources. See [01_PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md](../01_PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md).

## 2. Evidence labels

Use these labels consistently in articles and tables.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `[CANON]` | Unchanged established DanMachi canon. |
| `[RETELLING]` | A deliberate project-specific change to canon. |
| `[NEW]` | Original project material explicitly established or accepted by the author. |
| `[RETCON]` | An approved change to earlier project continuity. |
| `[OPEN]` | Unknown, unresolved, or unsupported. |
| `[OPTION]` | A proposal only; not continuity unless accepted. |
| `[REFERENCE]` | Information drawn from a supplied reference source. |
| `[UNVERIFIED]` | Rumor, suspicion, allegation, or unconfirmed report. |
| `[REJECTED]` | Rejected by the author; do not reuse as fact. |
| `[LOCKED]` | Explicitly established continuity that must be preserved. |
| `[CONFLICT]` | Sources disagree and the conflict remains unresolved. |

A label describes the **status of a claim**, not how interesting or likely it seems.

### Separate manuscript-status markers

Manuscript approval is a separate dimension from continuity evidence. Where applicable, preserve source markers such as `[DRAFT]`, `[DRAFT COMPLETE]`, and `[AUTHOR-APPROVED]` (or an explicit finalized/locked designation). A completed draft is not automatically author-approved, and an author-approved chapter does not make every theory spoken by a character an objective fact. Do not collapse these manuscript markers into the continuity labels above.

## 3. Article rules

- Cite the exact repository file and, where practical, chapter/section that supports each important claim.
- Keep a character's belief separate from objective narration. A suspicion is not proof.
- Keep rumors and allegations marked `[UNVERIFIED]`.
- Preserve intentionally unnamed identities. Do not infer names from canon expectations, appearance, or narrative role.
- Do not invent ages, dates, levels, Falna values, skills, spells, equipment, injuries, affiliations, relationships, motives, or outcomes.
- Do not let a character know information they did not witness or receive.
- Do not promote assistant-generated suggestions or drafts into established continuity without author approval.
- When a source is missing, mark the field `[OPEN]` and identify what source is needed.

## 4. Chapter status is not the same as continuity status

A chapter may be present in the repository while still containing draft or provisional material. Conversely, a continuity fact may be author-confirmed even if it has not yet been explicitly written into a chapter.

Record both dimensions where needed:

- **Manuscript status:** source archive, draft, approved, finalized, or not yet migrated.
- **Continuity status:** CANON, RETELLING, NEW, LOCKED, OPEN, and so on.

Do not call the entire Arcs 1–8 manuscript complete merely because source PDFs and continuity summaries exist.

## 5. Arc-specific restrictions

Character gates and exclusions apply exactly as written in their source. The currently documented Arc 8 gate explicitly permits Haruhime and excludes Artemis, Liliruca Arde, Welf Crozzo, Mikoto Yamato, and Mikoto's Familia members as a group. For Arc 8 prose, a FALSE/excluded character must not appear or be mentioned, including in dialogue, thoughts, memories, flashbacks, rumors, indirect references, off-screen events, or cameos.

This gate is Arc 8-specific. Do not automatically apply it to other arcs.

## 6. Updating a wiki article

1. Read the source file, not just an older wiki summary.
2. Confirm the relevant passage and its approval status.
3. Update the article and its source references.
4. Update [Editorial Status](Editorial-Status.md) if a gap closes or a new uncertainty appears.
5. Check linked pages for stale facts.
6. Preserve the manuscript itself unless the author separately requests a manuscript edit.
