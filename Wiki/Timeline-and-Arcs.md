## Verified chapter checkpoints

### Arc 3 — The Labyrinth City

- **Chapter 13 — “Lefiya”**: Bell intervenes in a carriage attack by kobolds and meets Lefiya.
- **Chapter 14 — “The Decision”**: Bell approaches the Loki Familia after difficulty finding a Familia; Finn challenges him.
- **Chapter 15 — “The Duel”**: Bell uses a barrier, Magic Missile, explosions, mobility, and improvised concussive tactics against Finn. Bell has no Falna at this checkpoint.
- **Chapter 16 — “Doubt and Regret”**: Bell declines the Loki Familia offer. Compatibility and his desired path are central to his decision.

### Arc 7 — Rolling the Dice

- **Chapter 46 — “Arson”**: The Hestia Familia church and Bell's laboratory are burned. The arsonist remains unidentified. Rumors implicate the Apollo Familia, but responsibility is not confirmed. Two challengers announce a War Game; their identities and the terms remain unresolved in this checkpoint.

## Chronology cautions

- Bell begins the retelling at age 10, but the active Arc 8 master command locks his age at **14 during the relevant later events**. Do not use the starting age as his later age.

- Do not infer calendar dates or elapsed time where the source does not state them.
- Do not auto-renumber duplicate or missing chapter numbers.
- Chapter Thirty-Three has two different titles recorded in the migration audit: “Vacation” and “Limits — I.” This requires source-level resolution.
- Chapter Thirty-Two was not found in the extracted heading structure.
- The Arc 1/2, Arc 4/5, and portions of Arc 6/7 boundaries require verification against the source manuscripts.

## Current manuscript storage rule

New chapters from Chapter 71 onward are stored in `Manuscript/`. Keep verified historical chapters in their respective arc folders. Do not move or delete historical files merely to make the directory look uniform.
