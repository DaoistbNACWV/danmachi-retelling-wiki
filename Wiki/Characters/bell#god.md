## 2. Copy Magic

**Classification:** Magic  
**Status in this profile:** Author-confirmed mechanics

Bell can copy another person's magic.

The copying process has no additional restrictions beyond the total cost of using the copied magic. Do not impose compatibility requirements, mandatory prior mastery, or a requirement to independently reconstruct the spell.

### Demonstrated example

Bell copied Ais's **Ariel**.

This is a demonstrated application of Copy Magic, not merely a theoretical capability.

### Resource context

Bell possesses the largest Mind pool and the largest mana pool.

He can also forcibly draw mana from the ambient environment or atmosphere, supplementing the mana available within himself.

Accordingly, the total cost of using copied magic is the relevant limitation. Do not invent other restrictions.

## 3. The Flow and Mushin

### The Flow

The Flow is a named skill recorded in Bell's Falna. Its described function is to perceive movement, momentum, intent, and the natural flow of battle.

### Mushin — 無心

**Classification:** Evolution of The Flow  
**Core principle:** The absence of rejection.

Mushin is not merely a state in which Bell stops thinking, nor is it ordinary imitation. Its defining principle is that Bell does not reject a technique simply because it is unfamiliar, foreign to his style, or developed through a discipline he has not practiced.

When Bell encounters a technique, he can accept it, perceive how it works, assimilate it, and refine it.

### Technique assimilation

The process can be understood as:

1. **Observation:** Bell sees a technique being performed.
2. **Non-rejection:** He does not dismiss or resist the technique because it is unfamiliar.
3. **Understanding:** He comprehends its movement, principles, and execution.
4. **Assimilation:** He incorporates the technique into his own repertoire.
5. **Refinement:** He adjusts and improves the technique rather than merely reproducing the original form.

Mushin therefore allows Bell to learn from another person's technique and make it his own. The result can be a refined version, not a strict copy.

### Reference comparison

The useful comparison is Garou from *One-Punch Man*: observing martial arts, understanding their principles, adapting them, and developing more effective applications.

This is a conceptual comparison, not a claim that Bell and Garou have identical powers or mechanics.

## 4. Distinction between Copy Magic and Mushin

| Ability | Domain | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Copy Magic** | Magic | Copies another person's magic. Its relevant limitation is the total cost of using it. |
| **Mushin** | Physical techniques and combat methods | Accepts, assimilates, and refines techniques observed in others. |

Do not conflate the two. Copy Magic reproduces magic; Mushin develops Bell's ability to learn and refine techniques.
