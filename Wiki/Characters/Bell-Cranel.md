## Overview

Bell Cranel is the central protagonist of *DanMachi: Retelling of the Legend*. This version follows a deliberately altered timeline and includes original project material. It should not be assumed to share every event, relationship, or power progression with the original DanMachi continuity.

## Retelling foundation

- **Starting age:** 10 years old in the retelling foundation. [RETELLING]
- **Early premise:** The Land of Dreams and a mysterious Being/assistant are part of the project's original setup. The Being cannot answer every question. [NEW]
- **Development:** Bell's mental growth through dream-time is part of the early premise. [NEW]
- **Prototype wand:** An early wand made from Forest of Wishe wood is recorded with estimated performance changes of +12% output, -5% mental consumption, and +40% effective range. These are project-specific estimates, not DanMachi canon. [NEW]

## Characterization principles

The author-established direction is that Bell should be capable without being invincible, intelligent without being omniscient, and strong without automatically becoming the strongest. He may think analytically and calculate risks, opponents, and compatibility, but he must retain believable emotion, flaws, and limitations.

His unconventional tactics, magic, mobility, and problem-solving should provide advantages without erasing experience gaps or the consequences of his decisions.

## Verified checkpoints

### Arc 3 — The Labyrinth City

At the Chapter 15 duel checkpoint, Bell has **no Falna**. He uses a magic barrier, Magic Missile, explosions, mobility, and improvised concussive tactics against Finn. He can surprise and damage a much more experienced opponent, but Finn's experience advantage remains substantial.

Bell declines the Loki Familia offer in Chapter 16. His reasoning concerns compatibility and whether joining that Familia would support the path he wants; it is not simply a fear of Finn's strength.

### Arc 8 — Crescendo

The Arc 8 Chapter 65 checkpoint records Bell independently entering the Dungeon, encountering Minotaurs from the fifteenth floor, destroying Goliath on the seventeenth floor, and reuniting with Finn on the eighteenth floor.

Chapter 67 records a spar with Ais. Bell uses Crimson Order, a rapier originally forged as a gift for Astrea. The spar includes Ais's Waldstein swordsmanship, Ariel, the black wind, and Bell's use of Dispel.

Chapter 75, “By My Words,” is marked CANON / AUTHOR-APPROVED in the manuscript index. Bell completes Ein's purification after Uranus's authorization, confirms that the observed biological structures remain intact, deactivates suspended animation, and Ein awakens.
