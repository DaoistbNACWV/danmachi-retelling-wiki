## Overview

Bell Cranel is the central protagonist of *DanMachi: Retelling of the Legend*. This version follows a deliberately altered timeline and includes original project material. It should not be assumed to share every event, relationship, or power progression with the original DanMachi continuity.

## Characterization principles

The author-established direction is that Bell should be capable without being invincible, intelligent without being omniscient, and strong without automatically becoming the strongest. He may think analytically and calculate risks, opponents, and compatibility, but he must retain believable emotion, flaws, and limitations.

His unconventional tactics, magic, mobility, and problem-solving should provide advantages without erasing experience gaps or the consequences of his decisions.

## Verified checkpoints

### Arc 3 — The Labyrinth City

At the Chapter 15 duel checkpoint, Bell has **no Falna**. He uses a magic barrier, Magic Missile, explosions, mobility, and improvised concussive tactics against Finn. He can surprise and damage a much more experienced opponent, but Finn's experience advantage remains substantial.

Bell declines the Loki Familia offer in Chapter 16. His reasoning concerns compatibility and whether joining that Familia would support the path he wants; it is not simply a fear of Finn's strength.

The Arc 8 Chapter 65 checkpoint records Bell independently entering the Dungeon, encountering Minotaurs from the fifteenth floor, destroying Goliath on the seventeenth floor, and reuniting with Finn on the eighteenth floor.

Chapter 67 records a spar with Ais. Bell uses Crimson Order, a rapier originally forged as a gift for Astrea. The spar includes Ais's Waldstein swordsmanship, Ariel, the black wind, and Bell's use of Dispel.

Chapter 75, “By My Words,” is marked CANON / AUTHOR-APPROVED in the manuscript index. Bell completes Ein's purification after Uranus's authorization, confirms that the observed biological structures remain intact, deactivates suspended animation, and Ein awakens. The chapter explicitly refers to Ein as Bell's girlfriend; see the [Ein profile](Ein.md).

### Copy Magic — confirmed rules

**Classification:** Falna magic.  
**Core mechanics:** AUTHOR-CONFIRMED.  
**Demonstrated example:** Bell copying Ais's Ariel.

Bell copies another person's magic. He does not need to independently reconstruct the spell, prove compatibility, or master the original beforehand.

The only established restriction is the **total cost of using the copied magic**.

Do not invent additional restrictions such as:
- A compatibility requirement.
- A requirement to master the original spell first.
- A requirement to research or recreate the spell independently.
- A fixed number of copies.
- A fixed duration.
- A rule that copied magic must be weaker than the original.

Bell has the largest Mind pool and the largest mana pool. He can also forcibly draw mana from the surrounding environment / atmosphere. Exact numeric capacities, extraction radius, extraction rate, and environmental consequences remain unspecified unless established elsewhere.

### The Flow and Mushin — technique boundary

**The Flow** is a named Falna skill in the Chapter 17 draft. Its recorded function is combat perception: Bell perceives movement, momentum, intent, and the natural flow of battle.

**Mushin (無心)** is a distinct advanced technique / evolution associated with The Flow. Its defining principle is **the absence of rejection**.

Mushin is not merely an empty mind, lack of thought, or automatic movement. When Bell observes a technique, he does not reject it because it is unfamiliar, foreign to his style, or from another discipline. He accepts it, understands it, assimilates it, and refines it.

Its conceptual process:

1. **Observation:** Bell sees another person perform a technique.
2. **Absence of rejection:** He does not dismiss or resist the technique because it is unfamiliar.
3. **Comprehension:** He understands its movement, principles, and execution.
4. **Assimilation:** He incorporates it into his own repertoire.
5. **Refinement:** He improves its execution, efficiency, or application instead of merely reproducing the original form.

The Garou comparison from *One-Punch Man* is a conceptual reference for observing, understanding, adapting, and refining martial techniques. It does not mean Bell and Garou have identical powers or mechanics.

**Copy Magic and Mushin are not interchangeable:**

| Ability | Domain | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Copy Magic | Magic | Copies another person's magic; the only established limitation is the total cost of using it. |
| Mushin | Techniques / martial and combat methods | Accepts, assimilates, and refines techniques Bell observes. |

**Mushin is sealed from current narrative use.** It belongs to the reserved Bell#God / future-state profile and must not be portrayed as active until the author explicitly says **"god mode"**. Its inclusion here documents the concept; it does not activate it.

### Rituals

Rituals are their own category. Do not relabel them as ordinary spells or chants.

**Divine Purification — Chapter 75, “By My Words”**

Author-supplied translation:

> “For the Heaven commanded, that I am. The Lord of light and darkness, the primordial death, the bringer of famine, the creator of war, and the path of conquest. By my words this mortal shall not be tainted nor corrupted. 'Divine Purification'.”

This is a ritual performed by Bell, associated with purification and the removal of corruption. The translation is author-provided story material; do not claim it is academically verified or invent unestablished mechanics, costs, or limitations.

**Rite of Three Severances — Chapter 69, “The Trap”**

The ritual separates three forms of influence:
1. Darkness.
2. Sickness.
3. Ill fortune / Dungeon influence.

It frees Ein / Filvis from the Dungeon's influence. It does not automatically heal physical injuries or erase psychological trauma. Do not treat it as a universal cure.

**Resurrection attempt — reference-recorded**

Project notes record three attempts: earlier attempts involving Bell's mother and aunt, and a third attempt involving Karina. Three silver coins were used in the Karina attempt; it failed, and Hades heard and recognized Bell. The exact mechanics and reason for failure remain unknown unless verified in the manuscript.

### Healing, reconstruction, and research

**Runic separation and reconstruction — Chapter 73, “Test One”**

Bell investigates separating corruption from affected tissue, removing the harmful influence, and reconstructing damaged biological structures. He recognizes that involvement of the heart or brain creates serious risks. This is research into the underlying structure of the problem, not simply an ordinary healing spell.

**Fels restoration research — Chapter 78, “The Fool”**

Bell plans to restore Fels's original body rather than create an artificial replacement. His considerations include muscles, connective tissues, nerves, blood vessels, organs, and bones. The plan and research direction are established; successful restoration is not established by the information recorded here.
