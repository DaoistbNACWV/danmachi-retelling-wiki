## Overview

Ein is the person whose corruption Bell investigates and ultimately removes in the Arc 8 purification sequence. Chapter 75, “By My Words,” is marked CANON / AUTHOR-APPROVED in the manuscript index and records her awakening after the procedure.

## Verified status and events

- **Before purification:** Ein is in suspended animation while an artificial network/corruption remains integrated with her body. The exact full mechanism and origin remain subject to the chapter evidence and unresolved continuity notes.
- **Chapter 73 — “Test One”:** Bell conducts a controlled extraction procedure and discovers that the remaining corruption is dangerously integrated with vital structures.
- **Chapter 74 — “Test: Permission to Use Arcanum”:** Bell obtains Uranus's authorization to use Arcanum for the purification procedure.
- **Chapter 75 — “By My Words”:** Bell completes the purification. The corruption readings disappear, the monitored biological structures remain intact, suspended animation is deactivated, and Ein awakens.
- **Relationship:** By Chapter 75, the manuscript explicitly refers to Ein as Bell's girlfriend. Their reunion and mutual affection are part of the author-approved chapter. Do not infer the exact date or chapter in which the relationship began.

## Identity and continuity cautions

- Chapter 69 is indexed as a standalone Bell and Ein/Filvis Chalia narrative and explicitly warns that the encounter is not automatically binding continuity for later Arc 8 chapters without author confirmation.
- Chapter 72 raises the relationship between two Filvis identities as an unresolved matter.
- Do not assume or invent the exact relationship between Ein and Filvis Chalia, their identities, aliases, or history beyond what an approved source explicitly establishes.
- Do not invent Ein's age, race, Familia, Falna, level, abilities, current location, or longer-term recovery status.
