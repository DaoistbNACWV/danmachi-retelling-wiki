## Overview

Fels, known as the Fool, is an eight-century-old alchemist and researcher who existed in a skeletal, wraith-like condition after losing their flesh. In Chapter 78, Bell restores Fels's body. With the restoration, Fels recovers the name **Senku Ishigami** and memories of his identity as a scientist and inventor.

This is an author-created crossover integration. Senku's appearance in this retelling does not make him part of DanMachi canon.

## Identity and pronouns

- **Before restoration:** Fels is not affiliated with a gender. Use they/them/their when referring to Fels in the skeletal state.
- **After restoration:** Fels remembers the identity Senku Ishigami and is presented in a male body. Use he/him for Senku after the identity recovery.
- The name “Fels” remains the name associated with the Fool and the centuries spent in the skeletal condition; “Senku Ishigami” is the identity remembered after restoration.

## Appearance after restoration

Chapter 78 describes Senku with spiky dirty-blonde hair tipped green, deep red eyes, and two distinctive diagonal scar-like marks across his forehead/eyebrows. He wears the tattered robe that remained after the restoration.

## History and research objective

- Fels created the Philosopher's Stone.
- The chapter attributes the loss of Fels's flesh and suppression of the knowledge tied to their original purpose to punishment by their god, Osiris.
- After restoration, Senku remembers that his driving objective was not immortality itself, but a cure for the petrification caused by Medusa.
- His recovered memories are incomplete in places. The exact extent of what he remembers after eight centuries remains [OPEN].
- Chapter 78 establishes that Fels descended to the seventieth floor in this AU. It names the related historical episode the **Seventieth-Floor Retreat**. Its date, participants, cause, details, and wider consequences remain [OPEN].

## Chapter 78 — “The Fool”

Bell visits Uranus's Prayer Altar with Ein and introduces her to Uranus. During the visit, Bell asks Fels whether they would choose restoration over continued immortality. Fels says they would have chosen restoration long ago.

Bell activates a restorative spell that had been prepared through Chant Delay. The spell reconstructs Fels's missing physical body, including the skeletal framework, muscles, connective tissues, blood vessels, nerves, and internal organs. On seeing his reflection, Fels recovers the name Senku Ishigami and remembers his scientific identity and Medusa-related research goal.

Senku experiences genuine tears and joy at regaining his body and purpose, then returns to his preserved laboratory to resume research.
