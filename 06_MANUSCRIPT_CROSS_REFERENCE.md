## 1. Canon corrections confirmed by the author

These corrections were explicitly supplied by the author during cross-reference review and take precedence over earlier uncertain notes in this pack:

- **Bell's age:** Bell is **14** during the relevant later events. The timeline is: age 10 at the beginning, then three years pass, followed by an additional time gap. Do not leave Bell's later age marked as unknown or assume he is still 10.
- **Erasure magic:** **Permanent Erasure and Existence Erasure refer to the same magic.** Do not treat them as two unrelated spells or as a confirmed progression between separate abilities.
- **Falna reference system:** Use the DanMachi Wiki's Falna system as the external framework for terminology and mechanics. This does not authorize inventing Bell's personal status values, current Level, skills, magic list, or Familia affiliation; those must still come from the manuscript and latest author-approved status information.

## 2. Core timeline and protagonist foundation

- Bell's story begins when he is 10 years old.
- The early material includes the Land of Dreams and a mysterious Being/assistant that cannot answer every question.
- Bell's body does not age in the Land of Dreams, while his mind develops through the passage of dream-time.
- The manuscript later explicitly establishes a three-year passage of time. The author's correction adds a further time gap, placing Bell at age 14 in the relevant later events.
- Later material identifies Bell as a god. Uranus subsequently identifies Bell as his great-grandson through the Zeus lineage. Keep these revelations distinct from the mysterious Being/assistant unless a chapter explicitly establishes a connection.
- Bell's intended characterization remains: capable but not strongest, intelligent but not smartest, observant and sometimes calculating, but flawed and capable of emotional attachment.

## 5. Falna reference framework

The author specified the **DanMachi Wiki Falna system** as the reference framework. Use the wiki pages below for general canon terminology and mechanics:

- [Falna](https://danmachi.fandom.com/wiki/Falna) — Falna as a deity's blessing; Status structure; basic abilities; excelia; status updates; rank-up requirements and effects; conversion between Familias; status sealing.
- [Development Abilities](https://danmachi.fandom.com/wiki/Development_Abilities) — development abilities and how they can appear from Level 2 onward.
- [Skills](https://danmachi.fandom.com/wiki/Skills) — inherent abilities discovered by those who have received Falna.

### Practical rules for this project

- Use the canon categories accurately: Level, Basic Abilities (Strength, Endurance, Dexterity, Agility, Magic), Development Abilities, Skills, and Magic.
- Distinguish ordinary excelia used to improve status parameters from high-rank excelia associated with rank-up.
- A rank-up is not automatic just because an adventurer defeats monsters; canon requirements and the deity's decision matter.
- A status update and a Level/rank-up are not interchangeable.
- Conversion changes the patron Familia affiliation while preserving the dependent's parameters, subject to the canon rules.
- Do not assign Bell a Level, stat values, development abilities, skills, or spell slots unless the manuscript or an author-approved status sheet supports them.
- Do not use the wiki to overwrite project-specific exceptions or author-established facts. The wiki is the baseline framework; the manuscript controls Bell's individual case.

## 6. Artemis continuity: original and replica

There are two distinct entities/iterations in the later story material:

1. **Original Artemis:** imprisoned within Antares, freed by Bell, and later subjected to judgment and sanctions.
2. **Artemis replica:** separately created by Artemis and gradually fading.

Never merge their scenes, memories, physical presence, or fate. A reference to “Artemis” must be resolved from the chapter's chronology and context before drafting.
